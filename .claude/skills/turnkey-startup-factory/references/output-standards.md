# Output Standards

## Candidate Screen

Use the same structure for every candidate so scores are comparable.

### [Concept Name]

| Field | Required Content |
| --- | --- |
| Keyword bundle | Four selected inputs |
| One-line concept | Customer + problem + solution + outcome |
| Buyer | Specific person or organization that pays |
| Purchase trigger | Event or condition that causes action |
| Core offer | Product or service and delivery mechanism |
| Business model | Price, billing basis, and likely expansion path |
| Acquisition wedge | First reachable channel and message |
| Differentiation | Specific reason to choose this over alternatives |
| Seven-day test | Cheapest credible demand experiment |
| Fatal-risk check | Legal, safety, privacy, platform, fraud, and regulatory issues |

Then provide:

| Rating | Score | Confidence | Positive Signal | Counter-Signal |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Profit Potential | 1–10 | Low/Medium/High | Evidence or explicit assumption | Strongest contrary evidence |
| Popularity Potential | 1–10 | Low/Medium/High | Evidence or explicit assumption | Strongest contrary evidence |
| Ease of Use/Learning | 1–10 | Low/Medium/High | Evidence or explicit assumption | Strongest contrary evidence |
| Composite | 1–10 | — | Formula result | Category-floor warning if applicable |

End with a one-sentence verdict: **Build**, **Validate**, **Hold**, or **Reject**.

## Launch Kit

Use the structure below for a winner that passes the build gate.

# [Business Name] — Turnkey Launch Kit

## Executive Decision

State the concept, ranking, gate result, most important assumption, and immediate next move.

## Business Design

Include the target customer, urgent problem, purchase trigger, product promise, positioning, category, differentiator, offer, pricing, guarantee, and upsell or expansion path.

## Product System

Describe the core workflow. Name four useful skills or capabilities and four integrations or plugins when relevant. Do not force integrations that add no customer value.

## Customer Experience

Write the first-use journey from awareness through first value. Include onboarding time, required data or permissions, learning burden, accessibility needs, support, and failure recovery.

## Brand Directions

| Direction | Palette and Mood | Audience Fit | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dark | Premium, technical, cinematic | — | — |
| Light | Clean, trusted, efficient | — | — |
| Rainbow | Expressive, inclusive, energetic | — | — |
| Pastel cotton candy | Friendly, playful, approachable | — | — |

Select one direction and explain why. Treat logos, marks, and names as provisional until checked.

## Conversion Copy

Provide a headline, subheadline, three benefit blocks, how-it-works sequence, proof plan, objection handling, pricing summary, FAQ, primary call to action, and secondary call to action. Do not invent testimonials, customer counts, awards, or partnerships.

## Go-to-Market

Identify the initial niche, acquisition channel, lead magnet or activation offer, content or partnership wedge, referral loop, sales motion, and retention mechanism. Specify the first three experiments with cost ceiling, success metric, and stop condition.

## Operating Model

Define recurring activities, owners or agents, inputs, outputs, review cadence, data handling, approval tier, failure behavior, and observability. Include a workflow written as:

> Subscription or request → orchestrator → specialist agent with selected skills and integrations → evidence and scorecard → approval tier → customer output

## Risks and Controls

Cover demand, competition, economics, product, operations, privacy, security, legal or regulatory exposure, platform dependence, and reputational risk. Map each risk to a mitigation, owner, trigger, and approval tier.

## Seven-Day Validation Sprint

Give daily actions, participant target, maximum spend, interview or landing-page script, success threshold, failure threshold, and the decision on day seven.

## Daily Run Record

Save a machine-readable or Markdown record with:

- Run ID and date
- Keyword-pool version
- Selected bundles
- Excluded recent bundles
- Candidate artifact paths
- Evidence sources and access dates
- Category scores and confidence
- Ranking and gate status
- Winner and output mode
- Assumptions and TBD items
- Approval state
- Next validation test
- Delivery destination

Never mark a run complete if a public or financial Tier 3 action is still awaiting confirmation.
