  ([shiftcare.com](https://shiftcare.com/us/blog/scheduling-software-for-healthcare-in-2026-what-home-care-agencies-really-need),

  employee** with ANSI-accredited incumbents (ServSafe, StateFoodSafety)
  ([orderout.co](https://www.orderout.co/blog/food-handler-certification-cost/),
  [coggno.com](https://coggno.com/blog/restaurant-food-service-compliance-training-requirements/)).
