## Evidence Notes (sourced 2026-07-28)

- **A:** AI receptionist pricing precedent is established at roughly **$99–$299/mo**
  flat-rate for SMBs; sources report **~62% of SMB inbound calls go unanswered**
  (higher in home services) and missed calls costing **$200–$1,200 each**
  ([phone2.io](https://www.phone2.io/post/ai-answering-service-cost),
  [getnextphone.com](https://www.getnextphone.com/blog/ai-receptionist-pricing-guide),
  [beancount.io](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/16/ai-phone-agents-receptionist-small-business-guide)).
  Counter-signal: crowded category (Smith.ai, Phone2, Allo, NextPhone…) — differentiation must come from a vertical wedge.
- **B:** Frontline LMS pricing precedent **$3–$8/user/mo** (e.g. Opus $8)
  ([opus.so](https://www.opus.so/blog/best-frontline-lms),
  [leap10x.in](https://www.leap10x.in/blogs/best-microlearning-platforms-frontline-workers/)).
  Counter-signal: incumbent-heavy (Axonify, YOOBIC), sales-led acquisition.
- **C:** Pet-care apps market ≈ **$3.48B (2026)**; household pet spend ≈ **$1,445/pet/yr**;
  ~68% of owners use pet apps
  ([businessresearchinsights.com](https://www.businessresearchinsights.com/market-reports/pet-care-apps-market-123827),
  [digittrix.com](https://www.digittrix.com/blogs/top-10-pet-care-apps-in-2025-for-busy-pet-owners)).
  Counter-signal: willingness to pay for *coordination* specifically is unproven; many free substitutes.
- **D:** Visible demand for queer-safe event spaces (e.g. Queer Social on Meetup; recording
  disabled for privacy) ([meetup.com](https://www.meetup.com/queersocial/)).
  Counter-signals: community events monetize poorly, and this overlaps **Mangasm's own
  event features** — better treated as a Mangasm feature than a separate startup.

## Winner — CallCatch Local

**Concept:** Subscription AI voice receptionist that answers missed calls for
**home-services small businesses** (plumbers, electricians, handymen), books the
job into their calendar, and texts the owner a summary — sold flat-rate
(~$149/mo, *assumption within sourced $99–$299 precedent*).

**Buyer:** Owner-operator of a 1–10-person home-services company.
**Purchase trigger:** Realizing missed after-hours calls are lost jobs.
**Gate result:** PASSED all six checks → verdict **build**.

**Portfolio synergy (recommendation):** this is exactly the first template the
**Luxury Mastermind AI Ecosystem Builder** needs, and **Helping.LLC** is the
in-house guinea pig: build once, use it for Helping.LLC, then resell the same
ecosystem via the factory pipeline (`docs/EMPIRE.md` §4).
